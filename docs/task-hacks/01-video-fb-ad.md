# Video for Facebook Ad

**Purpose:** Use when creating a short video for the FB ad (feed preview, teaser). The ad video hooks and points to the landing page video—it does not sell the session.

**Related docs:** [01-fb-ad-entry-point.md](../ui-ux/01-fb-ad-entry-point.md), [02-landing-page-video-flow.md](../ui-ux/02-landing-page-video-flow.md)

---

## Checklist

- [ ] **Hook in first 3 seconds** — Problem, pattern, or emotional struggle the audience recognises
- [ ] **Hint at perspective** — Brief reframe or “there’s another way to see it”
- [ ] **Bridge to video** — “The full explanation is in the video” (or equivalent)
- [ ] **Clear CTA** — “Watch video” / “Pozri video”
- [ ] **Short** — ~15–30 s for feed context; scannable, not a mini-lecture
- [ ] **Tone aligned** with landing page and funnel video
- [ ] **No session sell** — Ad sells the next step (watch video), not the reservation

---

## Key constraints

- **Do:** Hook → reframe → point to video → CTA. Ad is a bridge, not a brochure.
- **Don’t:** Sell the session, explain the offer in detail, promise outcomes the video doesn’t deliver, use vague or generic hooks that don’t match the funnel video content.
- **Ad promises; funnel video delivers.** Create curiosity and expectation. Don’t repeat funnel video content.

---

## Message structure (copy pattern)

1. **Problem / pattern / struggle** — One clear, specific thing
2. **Perspective / reframe** — Brief hint at another angle
3. **Bridge to video** — “Full explanation in the video”
4. **CTA** — Watch the video

---

## Notes / open questions

- Test video preview vs. static image in ad
- Best length for FB feed (15s vs. 30s)
- A/B test hook variations (problem vs. pattern vs. emotion)
