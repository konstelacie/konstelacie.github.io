# Video for Landing Page (Funnel)

**Purpose:** Use when creating the main landing page video. Delivers what the ad promised, builds trust, then invites to session.

**Related docs:** [01-fb-ad-entry-point.md](../ui-ux/01-fb-ad-entry-point.md), [02-landing-page-video-flow.md](../ui-ux/02-landing-page-video-flow.md)

---

## Checklist

- [ ] **Opening: the problem** (15–30 s) — Reconnect with the ad. Restate problem/pattern so viewer feels “this is for me”
- [ ] **Explanation of the mechanism** (60–90 s) — Why the pattern exists, how it works. Core value.
- [ ] **Short perspective / insight** (30–45 s) — One clear takeaway. Reframe they can use immediately.
- [ ] **Invitation to session** (30–45 s) — Session as next step for those who want to go deeper
- [ ] **CTA** (15–30 s) — Direct call to reserve. “If this resonates, reserve a session for 10 €.”
- [ ] **Total length** — ~2–4 minutes
- [ ] **Value first** — Real insight before the offer. Viewer gets something even if they don’t reserve.
- [ ] **End with verbal CTA** — Clear “Reserve a session for 10 €” or equivalent

---

## Key constraints

- **Do:** Create trust (real person, real explanation). Deliver what the ad promised. Give a small useful insight. Transition naturally to session invitation.
- **Don’t:** Bury value under long philosophy. Make the video feel like a long sales pitch. Go over ~5 min (increases drop-off). Hide or weaken the CTA.
- **Ad promises; video delivers.** If the ad says “explanation is in the video,” the video must provide that explanation before asking for action.

## Structure skeleton

| Section | Purpose | Approx. |
|---------|---------|---------|
| Opening: the problem | Reconnect with ad, “this is for me” | 15–30 s |
| Explanation of the mechanism | Core value, why/how | 60–90 s |
| Short perspective / insight | One takeaway, reframe | 30–45 s |
| Invitation to session | Next step for those who want more | 30–45 s |
| CTA | Direct call to reserve | 15–30 s |

**Total target:** ~2–4 minutes.

---

## Notes / open questions

- A/B test 2 min vs. 4 min
- Different videos for different problem angles (personalisation)
- Mobile vs. desktop layout for video and CTA placement
