# Sceneplays (FB ad + LP video)

**Purpose:** Sceneplays for paired FB ad video and landing page video. One theme = one FB video + one LP video. They follow each other: ad promises, LP video delivers.

**Related docs:** [01-video-fb-ad.md](01-video-fb-ad.md), [02-video-funnel.md](02-video-funnel.md), [01-fb-ad-entry-point.md](../ui-ux/01-fb-ad-entry-point.md)

---

## Conventions

- **Content language:** Slovak (user-facing copy)
- **File names:** English
- **1:1 pairing:** FB video hooks and points to LP video; LP video delivers the explanation
- **Ad promises; video delivers.** Don't repeat LP content in the ad—create curiosity.
