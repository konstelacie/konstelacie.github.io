# Remarketing User Paths

## Purpose of this document

This document describes what happens with users who visit the landing page but do not reserve a session. Remarketing is used to **re-engage users who were interested but not ready to reserve immediately**. The goal is to offer softer next steps and additional value without competing with the primary CTA on the first-visit landing page.

---

## Why remarketing is separated from the landing page

- **Landing page (v1):** Single action only. Reserve a session. No email capture, no chatbot, no secondary paths. Keeps the funnel clear and reduces decision friction.
- **Remarketing:** Can introduce softer next steps for users who have already seen the offer. These users are warmer; they know the context. They may need a different kind of nudge.

By separating remarketing from the landing page, we avoid cluttering the first-visit experience while still serving users who need more time or more information.

---

## Types of users who leave without reserving

Typical categories:

- **Interested but uncertain** — The video resonated, but they have doubts. "What if it doesn't help?" "Is this really for me?" They need more reassurance or clarity.
- **Emotionally triggered but not ready** — The content touched them, but they're not ready to commit. They may need time to process or a lower-commitment step first.
- **Curious but wanting more explanation** — They want to understand more before reserving. One video wasn't enough. They're looking for additional content or context.
- **People who want to ask a question first** — They have a specific question that the FAQ or page content didn't answer. They need a way to ask before committing.

---

## Remarketing goals

Remarketing can aim to:

- **Encourage a second visit** — Bring users back to the landing page when they may be more ready.
- **Answer doubts** — Address specific objections through targeted messaging or additional content.
- **Offer additional explanation** — Provide another video, article, or resource that deepens understanding.
- **Provide a lower commitment action** — Email capture, "ask a question," or "watch another short video" as a step before reservation.

---

## Possible remarketing actions

Examples of softer next steps:

- **Ask a question** — A form or channel where users can submit questions before reserving.
- **Get more explanation** — A secondary page with another video, article, or FAQ expansion.
- **Watch another short video** — A follow-up video that addresses common doubts or goes deeper on one topic.
- **Leave an email for additional content** — Newsletter, tips, or follow-up content. Lower commitment than reservation.

These actions are suitable for remarketing because they don't compete with the primary CTA on the first visit. They serve users who need a different path.

---

## Remarketing message patterns

Example message styles:

- **"Many people have questions after watching the video…"** — Normalises hesitation. Invites questions.
- **"Not everyone is ready for a session immediately…"** — Acknowledges that delay is okay. Offers an alternative.
- **"If something in the video resonated with you…"** — Speaks to the emotional connection. Invites a softer next step.

**Why these messages feel supportive rather than pushy:**

- They acknowledge the user's state instead of pushing for immediate action.
- They offer options, not pressure.
- They validate hesitation ("many people," "not everyone") instead of implying the user is wrong to wait.

---

## Relationship between remarketing ads and the main landing page

Remarketing ads may:

- **Send users back to the same landing page** — For users who just need a reminder or a second look. The page is unchanged; the user may be more ready.
- **Send users to a secondary explanation page** — For users who need more content. A page with another video, extended FAQ, or "ask a question" form.
- **Offer a softer interaction** — e.g. "Ask a question" form, email signup, or chatbot. The ad leads to a low-commitment action, not directly to reservation.

The choice depends on the remarketing segment and the hypothesis about why they didn't convert.

---

## Future remarketing experiments

Possible experiments:

- **Separate remarketing for different topics** — If ads target different problems, remarketing could match: different videos or pages for different segments.
- **Testing email capture** — Offer a tip, mini-guide, or follow-up content in exchange for email. Measure conversion from email list to reservation.
- **Testing chatbot or question forms** — Let users ask before reserving. Measure whether this increases reservations or provides useful feedback for FAQ refinement.
- **Retargeting by engagement** — Different messages for users who watched the full video vs. those who dropped off early.
- **Sequenced remarketing** — First ad: "Still thinking about it?" Second ad: "Here's another perspective." Third ad: "Ask a question."
