# 011 — Life Autopilot Assessment — Questionnaire Specification (v1)

## Purpose

This document defines Version 1 of the Life Autopilot Assessment questionnaire.

The assessment is designed to identify the current bottlenecks within a person's life system—not to assess personality, mental health, or diagnose psychological conditions.

The objective is to create recognition, clarity, and curiosity, leading naturally into the paid Life Autopilot Diagnosis.

**Wording:** Slovak question text was refined in v1.1 — see [`019-question-wording.md`](019-question-wording.md). IDs, constructs, reverse flags, and scoring are unchanged.

---

# Questionnaire Structure

Total questions:

**24**

Dimensions:

* Autopilot (6)
* Identity (6)
* Energy (6)
* Relationships (6)

Estimated completion time:

**3–4 minutes**

Question format:

Five-point Likert scale.

English (reference):

* Strongly disagree
* Disagree
* Neither agree nor disagree
* Agree
* Strongly agree

Slovak (production — see also [`017-assessment-content-sk.md`](017-assessment-content-sk.md)):

* Úplne nesúhlasím
* Nesúhlasím
* Ani súhlasím, ani nesúhlasím
* Súhlasím
* Úplne súhlasím

Scores map to **1–5** in that order (before reverse scoring).

---

# Design Principles

Every question should:

* describe an observable everyday experience
* measure a recurring pattern rather than a temporary emotion
* avoid psychological terminology
* avoid suggesting the "correct" answer
* feel relatable and immediately recognizable
* measure only one idea at a time

Questions should never imply that the respondent is broken or has a disorder.

---

# Scoring Model

Each question contributes to exactly one dimension.

Every dimension contains six questions.

Questions may be reverse scored where noted.

Reverse scoring prevents response bias and improves reliability.

# Notes for Version 1

This questionnaire is intentionally conservative.

Its purpose is not maximum psychological precision.

Its purpose is maximum recognition, clarity, and trust.

Questions should continue evolving based on:

* assessment completion data
* score distributions
* client interviews
* paid diagnosis sessions
* recurring patterns
* wording improvements

The questionnaire is expected to evolve continuously as the Life Autopilot Methodology matures.
