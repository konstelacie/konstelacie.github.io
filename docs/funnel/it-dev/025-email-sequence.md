---

# Overview

This document contains the first production-ready version of the post-assessment email sequence.

Each email follows the architecture defined in previous documents.

The objective is **not immediate conversion**.

The objective is to deepen understanding until the paid diagnosis becomes the participant's own conclusion.

---

| Email | Theme | Primary Goal |

# Success Criteria

The sequence is successful if participants finish it believing:

> "The assessment helped me recognize something important."

and

> "The Diagnostic Session feels like the natural next step if I want to understand why."

The emails should never create pressure.

Only progressively deeper clarity.
