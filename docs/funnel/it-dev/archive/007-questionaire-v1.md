# Life Autopilot Diagnosis — Questionnaire Implementation Plan

## Goal

The free questionnaire is **not** a personality test.

Its purpose is to help people move from:

> "Something feels wrong."

to

> "Now I understand what is happening."

The questionnaire should create an **Aha! moment** that naturally leads to the paid **Life Autopilot Diagnosis**, where the underlying causes are explored.

---

# Core Positioning

We are **not diagnosing people**.

We are diagnosing the **current bottleneck in their life system**.

This is a crucial distinction.

Instead of:

> "You are the High Performer."

We say:

> "Your current life system is primarily affected by an Identity Loop."

This feels analytical, temporary, and solvable rather than defining someone's personality.

---

# The Diagnostic Model

The questionnaire measures four dimensions that correspond directly to the transformation pillars of the business.

## 1. Autopilot

Measures whether life is driven intentionally or by automatic routines.

Examples of themes:

- repetitive days
- lack of presence
- living reactively
- operating without reflection

---

## 2. Identity

Measures how much personal value depends on achievement and productivity.

Examples:

- performance-driven self-worth
- inability to rest
- responsibility for everyone
- perfectionism

---

## 3. Energy

Measures emotional recovery and vitality.

Examples:

- recovery
- motivation
- emotional engagement
- mental exhaustion

---

## 4. Relationships

Measures emotional connection versus functional interaction.

Examples:

- emotional intimacy
- communication
- presence
- connection

---

# Questionnaire Design Principles

## Use observable experiences

Avoid abstract psychological statements.

Instead of:

> I don't know who I am anymore.

Ask:

> When someone asks what I want, I usually think about everyone else's needs first.

---

## Measure patterns

Avoid measuring temporary emotions.

Instead of:

> I feel tired.

Ask:

> Even when I have free time, I struggle to fully relax.

---

## Use present-day experiences

Questions should describe real situations people recognize immediately.

Examples:

- how they spend evenings
- conversations with their partner
- weekends
- work habits
- decision making
- recovery
- priorities

---

## Avoid diagnostic labels

Questions should never imply pathology.

The goal is understanding, not diagnosis in the clinical sense.

---

# Questionnaire Structure

Recommended length:

**20–24 questions**

Approximately:

- 5–6 questions per dimension

All questions use the same Likert scale.

Example:

- Strongly disagree
- Disagree
- Neutral
- Agree
- Strongly agree

---

# User Experience

The questionnaire should feel valuable before the final result.

Every few questions show a short insight.

Example:

> People who constantly solve problems often stop noticing their own needs—not because they don't matter, but because their attention is always focused outward.

These micro-insights:

- increase engagement
- build credibility
- create curiosity
- reduce abandonment

---

# Scoring Philosophy

Do **not** calculate one total score.

Instead calculate four independent scores.

Autopilot

Identity

Energy

Relationships

People can score high in multiple areas.

The system identifies:

- Primary bottleneck
- Secondary bottleneck

This feels much more realistic than assigning one personality type.

## 3. What This Means

Explain what the dominant pattern usually looks like in everyday life.

Focus on recognition.

The reader should repeatedly think:

> "That's exactly me."

---

## 4. Hidden Blind Spot

Explain what they usually overlook.

Example:

People with a strong Identity Loop often solve every external problem before asking themselves what they actually need.

---

## 5. Potential Long-Term Risk

Avoid fear tactics.

Instead explain natural consequences.

Example:

If this pattern continues, achievement often becomes less satisfying while emotional fulfillment gradually decreases.

---

## 6. First Small Step

Provide one practical action.

The recommendation should be:

- small
- achievable
- immediately actionable

Not life-changing.

# Naming Convention

Avoid identity labels.

Instead use system bottlenecks.

Recommended names:

## Autopilot Loop

Life has become highly efficient but increasingly automatic.

---

## Identity Loop

Self-worth has become strongly connected to achievement.

---

## Energy Drain

Recovery is no longer keeping pace with demands.

---

## Connection Gap

Relationships have gradually shifted from emotional connection to task management.

---

# Connection to the Paid Diagnosis

The free questionnaire answers:

> **What appears to be happening?**

The paid diagnosis answers:

> **Why is it happening?**

This distinction is essential.

The free assessment should identify the pattern.

The paid diagnosis explores:

- how the pattern developed
- unconscious drivers
- reinforcing habits
- personal history
- interactions between all four dimensions
- the highest-leverage intervention

The paid session is therefore a natural continuation rather than a sales pitch.

---

# Methodology Philosophy

Think like an engineer.

People are not broken.

Their current life system contains one or more bottlenecks.

The goal is not to label people.

The goal is to help them understand how their life currently operates and where the greatest opportunity for meaningful change exists.

---

# Future Evolution

The questionnaire is Version 1.

After the first 20–30 paid diagnoses:

- review score distributions
- identify recurring combinations
- improve wording
- validate patterns against real client conversations
- refine the bottlenecks
- continuously improve the methodology

The questionnaire should evolve from marketing asset into a proprietary diagnostic framework that becomes the foundation of the entire Life Autopilot methodology.
