# 012 — Life Autopilot Assessment — Micro-Insights (v1)

**Production Slovak:** [`017-assessment-content-sk.md`](017-assessment-content-sk.md) §6 — use that pack for implementation. This file remains the English methodology draft.

## Purpose

Micro-insights are short reflective messages displayed between question blocks.

They are **not** explanations of the user's score.

Instead, they serve four purposes:

* maintain engagement
* reduce questionnaire abandonment
* build credibility
* create small moments of self-recognition before the final result

Each insight should feel like an observation rather than advice.

The reader should think:

> "I've never looked at it that way."

rather than:

> "Someone is trying to teach me."

---

# Design Principles

Every micro-insight should:

* be 2–4 short sentences
* avoid coaching clichés
* avoid psychological jargon
* avoid diagnosing the user
* sound calm, thoughtful and observational
* apply broadly without feeling generic

Do **not** end with a call to action.

The assessment itself is the journey.

---

# Placement

Recommended placement:

* After Question 4
* After Question 8
* After Question 12
* After Question 16
* After Question 20

This divides the assessment into six manageable sections while providing regular moments of reflection.

---

# Insight 1 — Living on Autopilot

**Display after Question 4**

Many people don't consciously choose to live on autopilot.

It usually happens gradually.

Small routines become habits, habits become normal, and one day it becomes difficult to remember when you last stopped to ask yourself whether your life still reflects what truly matters to you.

---

# Insight 2 — Achievement and Identity

**Display after Question 8**

Being reliable, productive and capable are valuable qualities.

The challenge begins when achievement quietly becomes the main source of self-worth.

At that point, resting can start to feel less like recovery and more like something that has to be earned.

---

# Insight 3 — Energy Is More Than Rest

**Display after Question 12**

Low energy isn't always caused by doing too much.

Sometimes it comes from spending a long time disconnected from what restores you.

Recovery is not only about sleeping more—it is also about living in a way that gives energy back.

---

# Insight 4 — Connection Happens Slowly

**Display after Question 16**

Relationships rarely become distant overnight.

More often, practical responsibilities gradually replace curiosity, presence and meaningful conversations.

The relationship continues to function—but it may no longer feel deeply connected.

---

# Insight 5 — Life Is a System

**Display after Question 20**

The different parts of life constantly influence one another.

When one area begins to drift out of balance, the effects often appear somewhere else first.

Understanding the pattern is the first step toward changing it.

---

# Tone Guidelines

Every insight should feel like it comes from someone who has carefully observed human behaviour—not someone trying to motivate or persuade.

Preferred tone:

* calm
* thoughtful
* insightful
* reassuring
* analytical

Avoid:

* inspirational quotes
* toxic positivity
* motivational language
* fear-based messaging
* promises of transformation

---

# UX Recommendations

Each insight should appear on its own screen.

Suggested layout:

Headline

Short insight

Simple illustration or subtle icon (optional)

Button:

> Continue

Allow users a brief pause before continuing the assessment.

---

# Future Personalization

Version 1 displays the same insights for every user.

Future versions may personalize insights based on previous responses.

Examples:

* stronger Identity indicators
* stronger Energy indicators
* stronger Relationship indicators

This should only be introduced once sufficient assessment data has been collected and validated.

---

# Guiding Principle

Micro-insights are not filler.

They are part of the assessment experience.

Their role is to gradually shift the participant from simply answering questions to observing their own life with greater awareness.

By the time the final results appear, recognition should already be unfolding.
